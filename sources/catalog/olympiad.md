## Not publicly available

- **UKMT olympiads, years before 2015** (JMO 1999-2014, Hamilton/Maclaurin 2003-2014, Cayley 2010-2014): paid bundles at https://ukmt.org.uk/product-category/past-paper
- **BMO / UKMT reuse permission**: papers are free to view, but https://bmos.ukmt.org.uk/home/usage.shtml forbids systematic public reuse and commercial use of solutions without UKMT permission — contact UKMT before shipping these in the app.
- **Australian MO** outside 2016-2020: AMT shop — https://shop.amt.edu.au/products/amo3 (2012-2019), amo2 (1996-2011), Book 1 (1979-1995); AIMO past papers also sold by AMT.
- **USAMO/USAJMO official solutions**: MAA-copyrighted and not published, apart from the journal reports (draft PDFs 2023-2025 at campus.lakeforest.edu/trevino/). MAA permission needed for problem text reuse.
- **USAMTS Years 1-9**: in the AMT books *USA Mathematical Talent Search* (Berzsenyi).
- **CMO Qualifying Repêchage**: not posted (page 404) — ask competitions@cms.math.ca.
- **Balkan MO / JBMO 1984-2023**: MASSEE archive removed in its redesign; only AoPS community copies remain.
- **MEMO** years whose host sites expired: use web.archive.org copies of memoYYYY sites.
- **RMM 2024-2026 solutions**: linked on rmms.lbi.ro but return 404.
- **Sharygin**: public, but geometry.ru did not resolve from this network — download from another network.
- **Tournament of Towns 1980-2015** in English: Toronto archive (math.toronto.edu/oz/turgor) returned 403; AMT published ToT books.
- **Not in English**: Iberoamerican (Spanish/Portuguese), Kürschák (Hungarian), All-Russian (Russian), China MO (Chinese).
- **MOP**: internal, not public.
