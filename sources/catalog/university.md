## Not publicly available

- **Indiana Intercollegiate Mathematics Competition (ICMC Indiana)**: Books only: MAA 'A Friendly Mathematics Competition' (1966-2000) and a 2024 Springer volume (2001-2023). Only the 2004 problems are on the website. (https://www.maa.org/)
- **NYU Courant written exams**: Email grad_math@nyu.edu (https://math.nyu.edu/)
- **Berkeley prelims before Fall 2022**: bmail-login resource folder for Berkeley students (https://math.berkeley.edu/graduate/phd-program/preliminary-exam)
- **Georgia Tech comp answers after 2015**: Department intranet only (https://math.gatech.edu/graduate/past-comprehensive-exams)
- **Iranian university olympiad**: No official English version found; Persian only through the organisers
- **Russian/Soviet student olympiads**: No official English archive found
- **CIIM (Ibero-American university olympiad)**: Spanish only
- **Chinese Mathematics Competition (CMC)**: Chinese only, with no official archive
- **Korean university math competition (KMS)**: Korean only
- **Putnam 1938-1984 and solutions 1985-1994**: Books: MAA 'The William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition' volumes (Gleason et al.; Kedlaya, Poonen, Vakil); official solutions in the American Mathematical Monthly (JSTOR) (https://maa.org/putnam/)
- **Older GRE Math subject tests (GR8767, GR9367, GR9768, GR0568)**: Retired ETS tests; not officially free (only the GR3768 practice book is) (https://www.ets.org/gre/)
- **IIT JAM MA answer keys 2012-2025**: Formerly posted on each year's organising IIT site; now removed. Try the Wayback copy of jamYYYY.<iit>.ac.in

## Notes

- Putnam: the problems are MAA-copyrighted ('further redistribution is subject to copyright restrictions'); the Kedlaya archive asks for links instead of reproduction.
- Downloads blocked on this network: vjimc.osu.cz (TLS proxy) and bolyai.hu (Anubis) were fetched through Wayback `id_` URLs; Harvard and Michigan (Cloudflare) are catalogued but not downloaded.
- Scanned PDFs: UCLA 181 of 361, Indiana 25, UT Austin 19, Schweitzer 1987, JAM 2013/2014/2017, GATE 2019/2021.
